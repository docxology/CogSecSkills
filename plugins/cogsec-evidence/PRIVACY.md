# CogSec Evidence privacy policy

CogSec Evidence is a docxology plugin for reviewing claims, their sources, and citations in Claude.

## What the skills read and why

The skills analyze excerpts and citation details from public sources that you paste into Claude for review. When attribution matters, the skills may read a credited author's publicly listed name and compare it with the citation or source excerpt. Paste the minimum text needed and remove private, sensitive, and unrelated personal details, including personal contact information.

## Storage and sharing

The plugin consists of Markdown instructions and listing metadata. It makes no network requests, uses no connectors, and creates no logs or separate records. The plugin sends no excerpts or analysis to docxology or to any service outside Claude. docxology does not receive your Claude conversation through this plugin.

Claude processes the excerpts and generated answers as conversation content. Anthropic handles processing and retention according to the terms and settings that apply to your account. See [Anthropic's Privacy Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy) and [Privacy Center](https://privacy.claude.com/) for details.

## Questions and reporting

For usage or privacy questions, use the [GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/docxology/CogSecSkills/issues) and share only synthetic or redacted text. For vulnerabilities, use [GitHub's private reporting form](https://github.com/docxology/CogSecSkills/security/advisories/new). GitHub handles the information you choose to submit there under its [Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
